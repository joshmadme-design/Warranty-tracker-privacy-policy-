Privacy Policy — Warranty Tracker

Effective date: 22 October 2026
Developer: Shahab · Contact: Shahabahmadkha@gmail.com

The short version

Warranty Tracker works fully offline and stores everything on your device by default. It shows no ads and uses no analytics or tracking. If you choose to sign in with Google, your warranty records and receipt photos are also synced to your private cloud storage so you can use them across devices.

Data stored on your device

  • The product details, warranty dates, and receipt/warranty-card photos you add are saved in a local database on your phone.
  • Expiry reminders are scheduled and delivered on your device (local notifications). Reminder data is never sent to a server.
  • Backups and exports you create are saved to the app's storage on your device and are not sent anywhere.
  • Uninstalling the app removes this local data.

Optional Google sign-in and cloud sync

If you sign in with Google (Firebase Authentication), the app uses your email address and a unique account ID generated for you to identify your account. When sync runs, your warranty records and receipt photos are uploaded to your private Firebase Cloud Firestore database and Firebase Cloud Storage bucket, protected by your account.

  • Cloud sync is entirely optional — the app works offline without signing in.
  • Sync runs only while you are signed in. Signing out stops syncing but does not delete data already synced to the cloud (see Data deletion below).

Permissions used

  • Notifications — used only for your on-device warranty-expiry reminders.
  • Internet — used only for the optional cloud sync described above.
  • Photos are added through the system photo picker and camera; no photo is accessed without you choosing it.

What we do not collect

No advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or third-party trackers are included in the app. We do not collect location data, contacts, or device identifiers, and we never sell your data.

Data deletion

To delete the data synced to the cloud, email Shahabahmadkha@gmail.com from the address you signed in with and ask for deletion — your warranty records and photos will be removed from the cloud database and storage. Local data on your phone is removed by uninstalling the app or clearing its data.
